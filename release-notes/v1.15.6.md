# RigOne 1.15.6 — Sharing, boards and disk cleanup

*Released on 2026-11-08.*

Sharing and boards that work, and a disk that gets cleaned.

- Sharing an agent, skill or workflow no longer fails when its text mentions a folder on your Mac or something that looks like a key. RigOne replaces your home folder with `~` and removes secret-looking text before anything is sent.
- An old board moves to the server in full. A task that cannot be sent no longer stops the move, and a retry finishes the same board instead of making a second one.
- Larger shares and boards: up to 1024 files and 16 MB per file in a share, and bigger task descriptions and comments.
- Storage shows the space RigOne really takes on disk. Evidence of finished runs and goals is removed after 3 days, large files as soon as they finish, and a Clean up now button frees space at once.
- The Skills screen shows a narrow list and a full reader again, and the organization library stays out of the way until something is shared.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
