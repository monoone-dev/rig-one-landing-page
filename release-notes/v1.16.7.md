# RigOne 1.16.7 — Design review on screenshots

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Design review works on screenshots when the app has no live page.

- Desktop apps open the design review on the agent's screenshots instead of an empty preview.
- Pin comments on a screenshot and send them; the same agent fixes them while the review stays open, and new screenshots replace the old ones, with before and after kept.
- The review always says what changed and where to look in the app.
- When a live preview fails or stays blank, the review switches to screenshots and says why; projects with a real web page can switch between Live and Screenshots.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
