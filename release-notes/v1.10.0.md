# RigOne 1.10.0 — One design system and a step you can review like in an IDE

*Released on 2026-10-25.*

This update moves the whole window onto one design system and keeps Auto goals moving on their own.

- Every screen now uses the same components, so controls, menus, dialogs and notices look and behave the same everywhere. The run bar is lighter, with one panel toggle and a settings popover.
- An Auto goal keeps going when its lead makes no progress, and a review step that only reads files no longer stops the work after it when it reports a problem.
- When Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode updates itself while a step or the lead starts, RigOne waits up to two minutes for it to come back instead of failing, and tells you plainly if it does not.
- Plain headers from imported plugin connections now reach your agents.
- Starting, polling and saving do less repeated work, so the window and long runs stay responsive.
- Many smaller fixes to the run screen, step previews and Remote notifications.
- A finished step reads like a review in an IDE: its changed files, hunks and lines, comments on a line that go back to its agent, saved versions you can return to, the functions it changed and where they are used, a preview of the app started from its folder, a link that opens a file at its line in your editor, and what the step cost.
- Everything from 1.9.1 is included.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
