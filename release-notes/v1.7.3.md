# RigOne 1.7.3 — Checks that pass the way they do in your terminal

*Released on 2026-10-20.*

## Checks

- **A project's full check passes inside a RigOne check step the same way it does in your
  terminal.** Steps no longer stumble over files RigOne keeps out of step copies, over several
  GitHub accounts signed in on one Mac, or over a check that needs its Git history.
- **The Lead starts in projects with long folder paths.** Its private connection no longer fails
  on macOS's limit for socket paths.
- **A host check can be retried from its saved copy.**

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
