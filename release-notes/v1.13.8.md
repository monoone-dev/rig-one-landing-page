# RigOne 1.13.8 — A Lead keeps working while it waits for your decision

*Released on 2026-10-31.*

A Lead keeps working while it waits for your decision, and the screen says only what RigOne knows.

- In an Auto goal, a card asking whether to stop or continue a run no longer holds the Lead. The run keeps going until you answer.
- A Claude Lead that has gone silent is stopped after 90 minutes, even while its work is running, and the screen says why.
- The Lead's activity line stays visible beside a pinned card, unless the Lead is waiting for that answer.
- The notice about a message sent during a long command now says only that RigOne passed it on, because the Lead may not have read it yet.
- When a Codex Lead's long wait is refused, it answers you in plain words.

To update from 1.13.7, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
