# RigOne 1.2.3 — Auto uses your Connections without asking

*Released on 2026-10-08.*

In Auto, the Lead can now give any step any Connection you have turned on, even one its role does
not list, for example the Playwright browser for a tester or Figma for a designer. RigOne no longer
stops to ask you for approval first. Auto means Auto. Steps get every Connection you turned on by
default, also the steps of a saved workflow Auto reuses when they name no Connections; a plan can
still name fewer or none. If you turn a Connection off while Auto works, the remaining work goes
on without it.

- **What it costs:** each Connection that runs on your Mac starts with every step, which adds a
  few seconds to that step's start. Turn off the ones you do not use, or let the Lead name fewer.

- **What still asks you:** writing outside a step's own copy. Approval is still once per plan.
- **What stays the same:** a tester that only reads still only reads, even with a Connection. A
  Connection you turned off stays off, and a plan that names it is refused by name.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
