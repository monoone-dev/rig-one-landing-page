# RigOne 1.14.10 — Two fixes for 1.14.9

*Released on 2026-11-06.*

Two fixes for problems found in 1.14.9.

- Closing one task tab stops only that tab's Auto goal. Before, it stopped every Auto goal in the same folder, so a goal started in another tab ended at once.
- Settings → Organizations lists your organizations again after RigOne renews your session, instead of saying they are unavailable for a signed-in account. When you are really signed out, it says so and offers to open Account.

To update from 1.14.9, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
