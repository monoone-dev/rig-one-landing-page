# RigOne 1.11.2 — Waiting goals stay awake after a restart

*Released on 2026-10-27.*

This update keeps waiting goals awake after a restart and lets Auto think as hard as the terminal.

- A goal that waits on a condition keeps being checked after RigOne restarts, even when it was saved before goals remembered their agent app, and asks you once its time limit has passed.
- Auto runs Claude steps at high effort by default instead of medium, so the same model works at least as hard as it does in the terminal.
- Everything from 1.11.1 is included.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
