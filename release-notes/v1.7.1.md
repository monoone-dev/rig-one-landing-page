# RigOne 1.7.1 — An operator for Auto goals and more projects that just work

*Released on 2026-10-19.*

## Auto

- **An operator for running goals (off by default).** When you turn it on, it watches your active
  Auto goals, notices when a goal is stuck or keeps failing, runs small checks on its own and tells
  the Lead what it found. It asks you only before anything that leaves your Mac or cannot be undone.
- **Stopping and messaging a run works while it is still being prepared,** and a goal keeps its
  place when you switch the model provider.
- **Checks that need tools on your Mac can run as trusted host checks,** and test fixtures stay in
  the step's own folder.

## Works with more projects and Macs

- **Python projects get their environment prepared,** and dependencies and project knowledge are
  picked up for any language, not only Rust and Node.
- **Tools and credentials in custom locations** (relocated config, cache and toolchain folders) are
  found and kept private.
- **Commits keep their full message and follow your project's sign-off rules.**
- **Light checks run side by side, heavy ones take turns,** and build tools share slots you can set.
- **Publishing works with HTTPS Git hosts other than GitHub** and with any push remote name.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
