# RigOne 1.13.0 — A calmer Agents & work panel, and a sound when a run ends

*Released on 2026-10-29.*

A calmer Agents & work panel, a sound when a run finishes, and runs that keep finished work and explain red checks better.

- The Agents & work panel follows the new look: quiet group headings, a card for each piece of work and its status as a dot with a plain word.
- An opened piece of work shows what needs you and what went wrong first, then the summary, notes, details and actions. Checks, changed files and workflow changes read as matching lists.
- RigOne can play a sound when a run ends or an Auto goal is finished. Settings → Runs has a Sound section to turn it on or off, pick one of five sounds and hear it.
- Finished work from the author is kept when preparing a check or saving its evidence fails, and those cases are no longer reported as failing tests.
- Required tests stay tied to the right check and keep their proof when a check is reused. A reused result is dropped when the tools a check runs have changed.
- A conversation keeps its text after the agent app restarts, and a button answer you gave is kept once.
- A connection you chose stays required, while tools the folder only inherits are reported as unavailable without blocking unrelated work.
- Auto's messages reach a Codex Lead that is waiting on a long command, during that same turn, instead of after it.
- When a long check fails, the step that gets the work back sees the end of the check's output, where it says why it failed, instead of the first lines of the build log.
- Preparing a run is faster: identity checks are reused, and only Cargo work waits for the shared build lock.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
