# RigOne 1.32.0

*Released on 2026-11-27.*

## Organize tasks into spaces and folders

- Group tasks and teams in the side panel into spaces and folders. Create them from the side panel, name them and nest folders inside a space.
- Drag a task or team onto a space or folder to move it, or start a new task or team directly in the place you choose.
- Fold spaces and folders you are not using. Removing a folder keeps its tasks and teams and moves them to No folder.
- The layout is kept per project on this Mac.

## Tidy up task names

- Tidy up tasks proposes a short name for each task in a project. Check the proposals, edit any of them, then rename only the tasks you choose.
- Nothing changes until you confirm. Tasks that changed in the meantime keep their newer names.
- Undo the last tidy-up to bring the previous names back.

Requires macOS 13 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later).
