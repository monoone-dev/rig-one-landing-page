# RigOne 1.16.8 — Every design review opens on screenshots

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Every design review opens on screenshots.

- Opening a design review shows the agent's screenshots right away; nothing compiles and no sign-in page appears.
- The agent captures each screen signed in, with realistic data, and handles sign-in in its own copy.
- The live page starts only when you click Live, in projects that have a web page.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
