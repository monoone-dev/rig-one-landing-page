# RigOne 1.14.2 — Model guidance for Auto

*Released on 2026-11-01.*

Auto picks models with better guidance, and the phone never sends a message it said was not saved.

- A goal's Lead gets guidance on models: a lighter one suits ordinary implementation, a heavier one suits planning, checking risky work and failure paths. It is a hint, and your choice always wins.
- If the phone cannot save a message, nothing is left that a reload could send. The words stay in the field with the reason.

To update from 1.14.1, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
