# RigOne 1.16.2 — Invitations that arrive

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Invitations that arrive.

- People you invite to an organization now get an email telling them to sign in to RigOne with that address and accept in Settings, Organizations.
- Inviting again after the organization changed no longer fails every time, and only owners see the Admin role when inviting.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.

## The package

`RigOne_1.16.2_aarch64.dmg` — macOS 13+, Apple Silicon.
