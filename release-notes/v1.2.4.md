# RigOne 1.2.4 — Auto works like an agent in your terminal

*Released on 2026-10-09.*

Auto steps and the Lead now run with full access, the same way `claude --dangerously-skip-permissions`
or `codex --yolo` runs in your terminal: no fence around the step's copy, no approval prompts, and
your normal environment, so a step can run your whole test suite, use your keychain and sign a
release. A **Skip permissions** switch next to the agent picker (only in Auto, on by default) turns
this on or off for the whole Auto run, the Lead and every step; Settings → Auto holds the same switch. Your GitHub account, the `deny` rules of your project and
keeping secrets out of command lines stay as they were.

- **Messages reach the Lead at once.** A message you send while the Lead is working now goes into
  its current turn instead of waiting for the turn to end, and the box no longer sits on "Sending
  to the agent".
- **Honest verdicts.** A requirement the tester could not measure is no longer reported as an
  infrastructure failure. Each part counts what was verified, what cannot be verified here and
  what failed, and the Lead cannot re-run an unchanged check on a part that is waiting for you.
- **Plan limits.** Usage shows each vendor's session and weekly limits as bars instead of one
  monthly total.
- **The stream keeps its history.** Older activity that left the view loads back from the saved
  files, also for long Codex steps. Edit rows count changed lines from the patch, or show no
  numbers instead of "+0 −0".
- **Auto screen.** The Agents slider and "Warn at $" are gone from Auto, and spend is a caption.
  "Jump to newest" no longer covers the Connections box, and the message box was redesigned.
- **Diagnostics.** The export uses schema version 4 with named fields and a reason code for every
  failure. Conversations that finished no longer show as active, and a step refused before it
  started passes on its refusal instead of an empty answer.
- **Identity check.** Commits made in a standalone Auto copy are checked in that copy, so a run is
  no longer stopped with "RigOne could not check who made the commits of this run".

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
