# RigOne 1.7.0 — A MonoOne account and Remote rebuilt for iPhone and iPad

*Released on 2026-10-19.*

## MonoOne account

- **Sign up and sign in with a MonoOne account in RigOne,** the same account you use in IndexOne.
- **Short pauses instead of day-long locks.** After a few wrong codes RigOne asks you to wait a few
  minutes and shows when you can try again.
- **Resetting your password works at once,** with your recovery phrase, an email code or by
  starting over, and every error says what happened and what to do next.

## Remote on your iPhone and iPad

- **Rebuilt around Projects, Conversations, Boards and Needs you.** Chat normally with a project
  without starting Auto, and review and change board tasks from the phone.
- **Notifications on your phone** when something needs your decision, a review is ready, a goal
  finishes or fails, or a usage limit is reached. Tapping one opens the right card.
- **Review screenshots on the phone** and pin comments to exact spots, then send them as your answer.
- **Polish labels** across navigation and question cards.

## Auto

- **Runs several goals in one project at once,** sized to your Mac's cores and memory, and lets
  you change the limits in Settings.
- **Asks you before it keeps failing.** After repeated failures of the same part it stops and asks.
- **Tells a broken gate from broken code.** When a required check fails on code the goal did not
  touch, RigOne runs it once on a clean copy and says plainly when the gate or the environment is
  the problem.
- **Uses the commands your project declares** (CI steps, scripts, package.json, Makefile) instead of
  guessing, and reads nextest, pytest, go test and JUnit results.
- **Checks less, finishes more.** One check runs at a time on the Mac, successful checks are reused
  on the same code, and the full gate runs once per batch instead of after every step.
- **Keeps evidence outside your repository,** and the Lead gets its own workshop copy for experiments.
- **Works with any trunk name and finds tools** installed by version managers such as mise, pyenv
  or Nix.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
