# RigOne 1.13.10 — RigOne cleans up after a step

*Released on 2026-10-31.*

RigOne cleans up after a step, and starts without false alarms.

- When a step ends, RigOne stops programs that step left running on their own, and tells you how many. Programs it cannot safely tie to that step are named, not stopped.
- Old runs whose results are already saved no longer write warnings to the log at every start.

To update from 1.13.9, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
