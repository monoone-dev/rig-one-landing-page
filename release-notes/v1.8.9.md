# RigOne 1.8.9 — Auto goals that carry on after they finish

*Released on 2026-10-23.*

This update keeps Auto goals going after they finish, makes Remote steadier and refreshes the look of the app.

- A message after a finished Auto goal continues that goal with its plan, sources and messages. Only New goal starts from scratch.
- Finishing a goal in a workshop with many files no longer stalls the lead agent.
- Remote keeps each conversation's history separate, so one slow conversation no longer holds up the others, and an interrupted device registration recovers.
- Secrets for Codex connections no longer appear in the process list; they reach the connection through its environment.
- The lead agent tells you when none of the member skills could be given to it.
- When RigOne cannot save a conversation, the message says why, such as a full disk, and the reason is written to the log.
- Checks that must confirm Playwright tests by name now read Playwright's results instead of saying the runner is not recognised.
- Surface is the default theme again on SurfaceOne 0.23; a saved Airy choice opens on Surface.
- Every screen uses the full width of a wide window, and empty pages all show the same centred card with the next step.
- Home and Generate project setup are icons in the narrow rail, and the task panel starts with Tasks and Boards.
- A board task opens as a modal centred on the window; a click outside or Escape goes back to the board.
- The branches popover is narrower, refreshes from an icon and shows where each branch lives as an icon.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
