# RigOne 1.16.6 — Board people from your organization

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Board people come straight from your organization.

- Board settings have a People section: the owner, everyone with access and their role (Admin, Editor, Viewer), with Remove next to each person.
- Add people picks members of the board's organization; nobody needs an invitation or an email address any more.
- A private board offers Share with an organization, which moves it into one of your organizations.
- Handing a board to another owner lives in the general board settings, behind a confirmation.
- Board invitations are gone from the app.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
