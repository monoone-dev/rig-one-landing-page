# RigOne 1.6.5 — Remote access works from your iPhone

*Released on 2026-10-16.*

- **Removing a phone no longer loops.** Revoking a device sent the same request to the server
  again and again until the server refused everything. A device is now revoked once, and a
  revoke that came from the server is never sent back.
- **A paired phone stays paired.** After pairing, the phone no longer falls back to the pairing
  screen on a slow start, a dropped connection or a refused refresh. It shows "Waiting for your
  Mac…" and connects when the Mac is back. Only removing the phone on the Mac, or unpairing on
  the phone, forgets the pairing.
- **Pair the Home Screen app.** The Mac shows a "Copy link" button next to the code, and the
  phone has "Paste pairing link", so the app you added to your Home Screen can be paired too.
- **Face ID starts from your tap.** iOS no longer quietly refuses Face ID as cancelled.
- **Fewer locks.** Short relay drops and token refreshes keep the phone's session and unlock.
- **Safer by default.** Sending a message to a thread, typing your own answer, replaying a step
  and restoring a result now ask for a fresh Face ID. The 15-minute unlock counts from Face ID
  and no longer extends with use.
- **Signing out and back in keeps Remote working.** Turn Remote access off and on after changing
  accounts and the Mac registers again; old pairings are retired, so pair your phone again.
- **Large replies and screenshots reach the phone.** Big screenshots are shrunk for Remote and
  sends are paced, so the relay no longer drops them.
- **Clearer messages.** A code another phone already used, a Mac that is offline, and slow
  actions ("Still working…") each say what is happening.
- **Steadier checks.** Two tests that failed under heavy load now wait correctly.

After updating, remove the old phones in Settings → Remote access and pair your iPhone again.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
