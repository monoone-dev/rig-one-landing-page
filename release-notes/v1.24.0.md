# RigOne 1.24.0 — Conversation history and storage

*Released on 2026-11-14.*

- Browse and search past conversations for the current project, then reopen a saved session.
- Conversations receive clearer generated titles after their first completed reply, while names you set yourself are kept.
- Settings → Storage breaks down disk usage and offers cleanup in one place.
- Completed Auto goals reclaim temporary working copies to reduce retained storage.

To update, open Settings → About. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
