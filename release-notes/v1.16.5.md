# RigOne 1.16.5 — Design review in the live session

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Design review happens only in the live design session.

- The old design card (screenshot, comment, wait for a fix) is gone; every design review in Auto opens the live design session.
- Clicking "1 needs you" opens the design review when that is the only thing waiting.
- When the app preview cannot start, the session still opens, says why, and offers Try again and Approve, so a goal never stalls on a design.
- Turning on "approve everything" also approves designs that are already waiting.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
