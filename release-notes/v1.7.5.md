# RigOne 1.7.5 — Lead messages that name their real sender

*Released on 2026-10-20.*

Messages sent by the Lead now identify their actual sender.

- Fixed Lead messages to workflow agents appearing as messages written by you.
- Kept your own messages labeled as yours.
- Used a neutral label for older messages without recorded sender information.
- Fixed sender labels in mobile activity and saved conversation history.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
