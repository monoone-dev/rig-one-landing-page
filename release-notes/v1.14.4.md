# RigOne 1.14.4 — Design sketches and live design review

*Released on 2026-11-02.*

Auto designs screens faster: you can pick between sketches, comment on a live page, and your remarks go straight to the agent that made the screen.

- A new screen can start as three sketches in the real app. You choose one on a single card, and the build starts from it.
- Remarks on a design card go to the agent that made the screen, in the same session, without a new run. Checks run once, after you accept.
- A design review can open the screen live. Click an element, write a note, and send your notes together.
- Every interface task now starts from a short brief of one screen and is looked over by a second agent before you see it.
- After you reject a design twice, the lead is told to rebuild the screens instead of patching them.
- A result the lead recorded early stays recorded when its run reports the end.
- The phone explains a process that has left a command behind.

To update from 1.14.3, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
