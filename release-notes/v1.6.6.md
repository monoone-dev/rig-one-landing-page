# RigOne 1.6.6 — your iPhone stays paired after a reload

*Released on 2026-10-16.*

- **The pairing survives a reload.** Safari could not read back one of the phone's saved keys,
  so every reload or reopen showed the pairing screen again. The phone now stores its keys in a
  form Safari reads back, and checks each save right away.
- **Home opens right after Face ID.** The phone no longer waits for every list before showing
  Home; projects, inbox, usage and your Mac fill in as they arrive. While it checks with your
  Mac it says "Checking with your Mac…", and any problem after Face ID is shown on screen.
- **A pairing the phone cannot open says so.** Instead of silently starting over, the phone
  shows "This device could not open its pairing" with "Pair again".
- **Diagnostics on the phone.** "This device" and the setup screen have "Show diagnostics" with
  a Copy button, listing the last steps without any keys or tokens.
- **A brief relay hiccup no longer ends the phone's session.** The Mac pauses for a moment and
  carries on.

After updating, remove the old phone in Settings → Remote access and pair your iPhone again in
Safari.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
