# RigOne 1.22.0 — Auto keeps going when a usage limit runs out

*Released on 2026-11-13.*

Auto keeps working when an agent app runs out of usage in the middle of a step: the same step carries on in the other app instead of stopping. Your answers to the Lead now reach every step, and the Lead keeps its context leaner.

- When Claude or Codex hits its usage limit partway through an Auto step, RigOne saves the work done so far and continues the same step in the other app. The new agent picks up with the previous agent's last words and the list of files it changed. It only switches to an app you allowed for that goal, and the switch does not count as a failed attempt. If no other app may take over, the step ends with the reason and its work stays in the step's copy.
- An approval or answer you give the Lead on a card now reaches every Auto step with your exact words and the time you gave it, so you no longer have to repeat it. Answers given from a board are kept on the goal just like answers given in the window.
- Long terminal output no longer floods the Lead. Anything over 8,000 characters is saved to a file, and the Lead gets the first and last lines plus where to find the rest, so long goals stay on track for longer.
- The Lead starts with the smallest real end-to-end run, asks before doing more than you asked for, and checks the whole path again after a repair that did not work.
- Type @ in a conversation to call one of your saved project agents. It runs with its own settings, and its progress shows in the conversation and in Remote. Clicking a suggestion now picks the row you clicked.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
