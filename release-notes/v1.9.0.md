# RigOne 1.9.0 — The redesigned Remote on your phone and tablet

*Released on 2026-10-24.*

This update brings the redesigned Remote to your phone and tablet.

- Remote has a new workspace layout: projects are easier to move between, conversations read more comfortably, and you choose how a new conversation starts.
- The message field sits where your thumb reaches it on a phone, stays clear of the keyboard and attached photos, and keeps your draft while you move around.
- Long names, large text and small screens keep their full labels instead of overlapping.
- Everything from 1.8.9 is included.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
