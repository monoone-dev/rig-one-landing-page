# RigOne 1.20.0 — Auto keeps going and tells you when it waits

*Released on 2026-11-12.*

Auto keeps going instead of standing still: a stuck step moves to a stronger model or the other agent app, a waiting goal tells you, and quitting or updating says what it would cut off.

- A Lead conversation keeps its lead agent after a goal finishes, after you reopen a saved conversation and after every reply, so "Pick a lead agent first" no longer appears when one is already chosen.
- A step whose cheap model gave up within five minutes moves to a stronger model on the next try.
- A goal whose final verification you excluded can now complete; the gap is recorded as accepted, and its text says what did not pass.
- Quit and Update now say how many minutes of running work they would cut off, and offer Quit when nothing runs and Update when nothing runs.
- A build that is short on disk space waits for cleanup instead of failing, and measurements no longer hold the shared checks slot.
- An author that reports "incomplete" without a concrete blocker is asked to keep going in the same session, up to twice. Every Auto step commits its work.
- When a step cannot start because its agent app is out of usage, it moves to the other agent app.
- A waiting goal notifies your phone and Mac, wakes its Lead when usage resets and restarts a stopped Lead. Lead memory trims itself.
- Haiku, Terra and Luna get only small parts; a plan that gives them more is refused.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
