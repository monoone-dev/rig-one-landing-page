# RigOne 1.13.1 — A rebuilt phone app for Remote, and steadier boards

*Released on 2026-10-29.*

A rebuilt phone app for Remote access, and boards that show what each agent is doing.

- Remote on the phone has a new layout: a simpler start screen and project picker, a clearer header and navigation, and long rows that wrap instead of being cut off.
- Conversations on the phone keep their place and their history when you leave and come back, and review answers, questions and approvals work by touch.
- Boards connect Auto progress, task runs and agent replies, so a board shows what each agent is doing and what it answered.
- The Polish description of the finish sound reads consistently.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
