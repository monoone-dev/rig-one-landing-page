# RigOne 1.11.3 — Finished goals and diagnostics tell the truth

*Released on 2026-10-27.*

This update makes finished goals and diagnostics tell the truth.

- A goal whose result branch was landed with a squash merge now counts as delivered instead of showing unpublished work.
- When copying diagnostics fails for a known reason, the button says that reason in your language instead of a generic error.
- When your answer to the Lead cannot reach it yet, RigOne's log now names what holds it back.
- Everything from 1.11.2 is included.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.

`RigOne_1.11.3_aarch64.dmg` — macOS 13+, Apple Silicon.
