# RigOne 1.30.0 — Teams in your project

*Released on 2026-11-26.*

## Teams that are ready to work

- AI-created teams now receive the skills their agents need before the team is saved, preventing missing-skill warnings immediately after creation.
- Paste screenshots and files into team and specialist conversations with Command-V, or drop them into the composer. Preview and remove attachments before sending.
- Teams belong to a project folder and are available in its subfolders. Agents receive that folder’s project instructions as context.
- Close teams from the sidebar and restore them from the closed teams list.

## Models and effort for each role

- Choose a Low, Balanced, Max or Ultra profile when AI designs a team. RigOne uses the available model lists and project context to choose each role’s model and effort, following the same model rules as Auto.
- Review the reason for each choice and change individual models or effort levels before creating the team.
- Open a specialist’s conversation to change its model and effort for the next turn. Changes are checked before saving and preserve the agent’s other settings.

Requires macOS 13 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later).
