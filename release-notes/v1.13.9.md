# RigOne 1.13.9 — A line when a Codex Lead leaves a command unwatched

*Released on 2026-10-31.*

RigOne tells you when a Codex Lead leaves a command unwatched.

- When a Codex Lead's turn ends and RigOne has not seen its command finish, one line on screen says so. The line goes away when the command finishes or the conversation closes.
- On your next message the Lead is told about that command, and not to restart it unasked or to claim it has ended without seeing it.

To update from 1.13.8, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
