# RigOne 1.14.0 — Auto works side by side, and a message never gets stuck

*Released on 2026-11-01.*

Auto works side by side, the Lead stays lighter on long goals, and a message never gets stuck in the field.

- Pressing Enter always takes your message. It leaves the field at once, waits in the conversation marked as not delivered yet, and later messages follow in order. If sending fails, your text comes back with the reason.
- Processes of one goal can now run at the same time in one folder when they do not depend on each other and change different files. When one has to wait, the Lead is told why.
- A goal's Lead can hand reading-heavy work to an Explore helper and gets a reminder after reading a lot. The helper's words no longer appear as the Lead's.
- A goal's Lead now tidies up its memory while it is idle, before waking up gets expensive, and earlier if a goal grows very large. The conversation says when this is happening, and anything you send waits and arrives right after.
- After its memory is tidied up, a goal's Lead goes back to the goal's status before carrying on.
- Answers from Auto's own tools are much shorter, and the process you just recorded is always listed in the answer.
- Questions a Lead asked you survive a restart of the app.
- RigOne's own notices no longer switch the Lead's model or effort back after you changed it.
- A run's plan shows how long it waited for a Rust build and for a free folder.
- A Lead process that depends on others takes its starting point when it can actually start.
- The history screen has a new look.

To update from 1.13.10, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
