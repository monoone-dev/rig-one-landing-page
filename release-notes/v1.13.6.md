# RigOne 1.13.6 — Leads that keep working while you decide

*Released on 2026-10-31.*

Leads that keep working while you decide, and clearer reasons when something ends.

- In an Auto goal, a Lead's question card no longer stops its work. The Lead raises the card and carries on, the card waits for you, and your answer reaches the Lead when you give it.
- A Claude Lead that has finished is no longer stopped after 90 minutes as if it had gone quiet, and the window no longer says it is still thinking after it was stopped.
- A Codex Lead is reminded to answer you when it starts a long wait while your message is unanswered.
- A stopped run now says who or what stopped it, in the run, in History and to the Lead.
- A failed check says what failed and how it ended. A check that passed without naming its tests says that, instead of blaming the test runner.
- A step that needs macOS permissions to use the computer says which ones to turn on and where.
- Automatic clean-up frees more disk: old run inputs, Codex caches, and build caches left in evidence folders.

To update from 1.13.5, open Settings → About and install it. From an older version, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
