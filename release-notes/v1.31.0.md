# RigOne 1.31.0

*Released on 2026-11-26.*

## Choose how independently teams work

- Set Manual, Supervised or YOLO as the default for all teams, with an optional override for each team.
- In YOLO, teams can approve plans, scheduled routines and connected-service actions within their assigned goal. Automatic decisions remain visible in their history.
- Budgets, review requirements, failed checks and explicit refusals continue to govern the work.

## Read chat while reviewing approvals

- Approvals now open beside the team conversation, with their own scroll area and a close button.
- Completed actions stay in a collapsed history. Narrow windows reserve a separate area below the conversation so the message composer stays accessible.
- Open a pending decision from the team header and close the panel with Escape, returning focus to its button.

## Resources that follow the team

- Generated teams retain the Connections and skills selected for their roles, including reviewers, and show selected Connections before creation.
- RigOne checks required skills and credentials, and verifies direct MCP availability before saving a generated team. A missing resource produces a named error.
- Selected Connections remain available in separate team work folders and delegated project tasks. Access keys stay in their original private settings.
- Required skill bundles follow delegated work when absent from the destination; existing project-specific skills remain in place.
- Direct MCP servers and ordinary HTTP actions use their respective access routes, and generated rules respect the chosen autonomy mode.

Requires macOS 13 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later).
