# RigOne 1.6.3 — Remote control, a new look, a Lead with your tools, and steps that say what they wait for

*Released on 2026-10-16.*

- **Remote control from your phone.** Turn on Remote access in Settings, pair your phone, and follow
  and steer your runs and the Lead from anywhere, with notifications when a step needs you.
- **A new look.** RigOne follows the redesigned screens: the same features, laid out more clearly.
- **The Lead has your tools.** With full access, the Auto Lead gets your own MCP servers, and on
  Claude Code your signed-in Chrome. It is told exactly which tools it has, so it does not search
  for them, and when something is missing it says so and offers the quickest manual step instead
  of installing software.
- **A Lead that is easier to talk to.** Short, answer-first messages in your language; long ids are
  shortened with the full value one click away; repeated notes appear once; a message sent around
  Stop reaches the Lead once.
- **Steps say what they wait for.** A step waiting for free memory, the CPU, the heavy-work slot, a
  Rust build, a usage-limit reset or earlier steps says which, with numbers and since when, and a
  wait longer than 30 minutes is flagged. Settings shows how much memory a heavy step needs.
- **Usage limits pause instead of hanging.** A step that reaches Claude Code's or Codex's usage
  limit pauses with the reset time and continues by itself at the reset; one app's limit no
  longer holds the other app's steps.
- **Clearer results.** A check whose program could not be found says so instead of reporting a
  code failure, a loop round that did not pass says it went back for another try, and the
  diagnostics export is shorter, with a plain reason for each failure and the agent app versions.
- **Boards and agent apps.** Board tasks have details, short numbers and an agent activity log.
  Agent apps can be turned on with a switch, and OpenCode's settings file can be edited in
  Settings.
- **Pasted images are not refused without a reason.** A valid pasted image always reaches the
  agent, and an image that cannot be used says why.
- **No keychain prompts from test builds.** Development and test builds keep their own keychain
  items, so they never ask for the installed app's passwords.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
