# RigOne 1.16.3 — Invite people to your own board

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Invite people to your own board.

- You can invite someone by email to a board that is not in an organization. They get an email, accept in RigOne and see the board; you see them in its members and can change their role or remove them.
- Board errors now say what went wrong in your language instead of a generic message.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
