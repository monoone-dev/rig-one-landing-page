# RigOne 1.19.0 — A simpler phone app and Auto profiles

*Released on 2026-11-11.*

A simpler phone app that shows only the questions meant for you, phone lists that stay correct, and a choice of how strong the models Auto uses may be.

- The phone app has one menu instead of tabs: the project picker at the top, then the project's conversations with the Lead first, then Overview, Work, Boards, Limits, Mac, This device and Settings. A project opens on its Lead conversation, and the Lead is marked in the menu and in the conversation header.
- The phone app has a dark mode: Settings > Appearance offers System, Light and Dark, and the app starts in your choice without a flash of the other theme.
- The phone app fills the whole screen down to the bottom edge, and Back on a run step returns to the screen you opened it from.
- The phone shows only the questions the Lead passes to you. Questions that agents ask inside their own runs stay with the Lead and no longer fill the bell, the questions list or your notifications. The Mac needs this version for that; with an older Mac the phone shows questions as before.
- The Mac keeps the phone's lists and conversations correct when a history file was written twice or an update to the phone got lost, so you see "nothing loads" far less often. The phone gets the current lists every time it reconnects, a conversation the Mac could not read comes back without restarting RigOne, and one unreadable conversation no longer stops the others from updating.
- A phone that is gone no longer slows down a phone that is connected.
- Auto has profiles: Low, Balanced, Max and Ultra decide which models Auto may give to each kind of step. Pick one in the agent popover; the choice is remembered per project, and Auto refuses plans that break it. Ultra also allows the strongest models for plans, analyses and final reviews.
- When the Lead stops, the conversation says why instead of only "The lead stopped again", and RigOne stops retrying at once when the Lead's saved notes cannot be prepared.
- Two goals changing the Lead notes index at the same time no longer create extra index copies that could stop every Lead from starting.
- Board comments get focus when you open them, and task progress stays under the Lead's control.
- Text in RigOne is a step larger and easier to read.
- One security advisory in a dependency is cleared.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
