# RigOne 1.8.8 — One agent, planning and Auto as separate modes

*Released on 2026-10-23.*

This update separates working with one agent from planning and Auto, and links conversations and plans to boards.

- One agent works directly with your project's skills and tools. Planning and Auto stay explicit modes you choose.
- Read-only reviews judge the evidence, and a step that truly needs your confirmation waits for your answer instead of failing the workflow.
- Conversations and plans can be linked to a board, with stable subtasks and progress updates, and an interrupted link recovers.
- Run all shows how preparation is going, and after a restart RigOne brings back saved conversations, their status and the selected agent.
- You can sign in to Codex MCP connections from the conversation and refresh their status without mixing conversations.
- Completed Codex edits are shown, and review tables read as accessible content.
- A check that must confirm tests by name now finds them when they run inside a module, instead of saying they never ran.
- tokio is updated to fix the I/O safety advisory RUSTSEC-2026-0386.
- The side panel can be resized by dragging its edge, pages start at the left edge, section titles sit level with their buttons, and Generate project setup is laid out like the other dialogs.
- Boards sign-in uses a centred card with the MonoOne logo.
- A Rust build inside a step no longer leaves the shared build cache broken for later builds on the Mac.

To update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
