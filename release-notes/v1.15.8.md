# RigOne 1.15.8 — A readable whole plan

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

A readable whole plan.

- The whole plan of a goal has its list of parts in its own column, so it never covers the graph.
- The plan opens zoomed in on the part you opened, and clicking a part in the list highlights its steps and zooms to them. Show everything returns to the full plan.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
