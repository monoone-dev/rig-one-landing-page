# RigOne 1.15.2 — Auto goals after a restart

*Released on 2026-11-08.*

You can write to an Auto goal again after a restart, and inviting a board member is quick.

- An Auto goal that was waiting when RigOne restarted keeps the lead model you chose. Messages in its conversation are no longer refused with "Pick a lead agent first", and a message is sent with the model the conversation shows.
- Inviting a member to a board sends the invitation right away instead of first syncing the whole board.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
