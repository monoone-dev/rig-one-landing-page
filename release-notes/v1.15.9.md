# RigOne 1.15.9 — Updates that never wait

*Released on 2026-11-09.*

Updates that never wait, and no extra word after a stop.

- Update now is always available. Running work pauses for the update: Auto goals continue on their own after the restart, and paused runs show Paused for the update with a Continue button.
- When the lead stops a part to replace its plan, it can start again right away. Only your own Stop waits for your next message.

To update, open Settings → About and install it. From a version without the in-app update, download the macOS Apple Silicon DMG once and replace RigOne in `/Applications`.
